How	to	read	this	report
Every	number	below	comes	from	a	strategy	that	was	tested	against
many	simulated	alternate	histories
,	not	just	the	one	5-month
sample	we	have	real	data	for.	This	matters	enormously:	across	this
entire	analysis,	dozens	of	strategies	looked	excellent	on	the	single	real
historical	sample	and	then	
failed	catastrophically
	
(50-97%	chance
of	wiping	the	account)	once	tested	against	resampled	alternate
versions	of	similar	market	conditions.	Only	four	survived	that	test.
Everything	in	this	report	is	one	of	those	four	—	nothing	here	is	a
strategy	that	merely	“looked	good	once.”
Monthly	ﬁgures	are	derived,	not	directly	measured.
	
Each
bootstrap	test	covers	a	~6-month	period	(126	trading	days).	The	“%
per	month”	ﬁgures	below	are	the	compounded	monthly	growth	rate
implied	by	the	6-month	median	result	—	i.e.,	what	monthly	rate,
compounded	6	times,	produces	the	tested	6-month	outcome.	These
are	projections	built	from	a	validated	median,	not	independently	re-
tested	month-by-month.

Critical	caveats	—	read	before	acting	on
any	of	this
1.	
All	bootstrap	testing	resamples	day-blocks	from	the	same
original	5-month	sample
	
(Dec	2025–May	2026).	This	tests
robustness	to	diﬀerent	
orderings
	
of	days	we’ve	already	seen,	not
performance	in	a	genuinely	diﬀerent	market	regime	(a	volatility
spike,	an	extended	one-directional	move,	a	structurally	diﬀerent
period)	that	never	appeared	in	this	window.	It	is	a	meaningfully
weaker	guarantee	than	true	out-of-sample	data	from	a	separate
time	period.
2.	
Transaction	costs	are	an	assumption
	
($0.35/oz	round-trip,	a
typical	retail/ECN	estimate),	not	your	actual	broker’s	conﬁrmed
ﬁgure.	Conﬁrm	this	before	trusting	the	exact	dollar/percentage
outcomes	—	these	strategies	trade	frequently	enough	that	real
costs	materially	aﬀect	the	result.
3.	
The	monthly	rates	above	are	compounded	projections	from	a
6-month	median,	not	independently	validated	month-by-
month	ﬁgures.
	
Real	month-to-month	results	will	be	lumpier	—
most	days	have	zero	trades,	and	a	small	number	of	trades	drive
each	period’s	result.
4.	
21-22%	monthly,	compounded,	is	an	extraordinary	implied
annual	rate
	
(well	over	1,000%	annualized).	Treat	this	as	what	the
validated	backtest	data	shows	for	this	speciﬁc	instrument	and
window,	not	as	a	guaranteed	or	even	likely	forward	rate.	Every
professional	benchmark	for	real,	sustained	trading	returns
operates	at	a	small	fraction	of	this.
5.	
None	of	this	has	been	tested	at	scaled	position	sizes.
	
All
results	assume	the	base	0.01	lot.	Earlier	analysis	in	this
conversation	showed	that	scaling	position	size	and	account	size
together	(e.g.,	0.1	lot	/	$2,000)	preserves	the	same	
percentage
risk/return	proﬁle	mathematically	—	it	does	not	create	a	better
rate,	just	a	bigger	absolute	number	at	the	same	odds.
6.	
Recommended	before	live	trading	(per	the	strategy	ﬁle
itself):
	
paper	trade	for	2-4	weeks,	track	every	trade	against	the
exact	rules,	and	ideally	validate	against	a	genuinely	separate
chunk	of	real	data	this	analysis	never	touched,	before	committing
real	capital.
